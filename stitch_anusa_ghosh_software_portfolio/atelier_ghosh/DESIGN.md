## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the synthesis of precise computer science and curated fine-art curation. Built specifically for an engineering student and technical creator, it abandons the sterile, neon-lit idioms of traditional tech portfolios in favor of an archival, museum-grade editorial presence.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Academic & Exacting:** Rooted in algorithmic discipline and architectural precision.
- **Warm & Tactile:** Evoking archival art papers, heavy-stock monograph prints, and hand-bound catalogs.
- **Discerning & Quietly Confident:** Generous negative space, deliberate typographic tension, and high-fidelity restraint.

### Visual Grammar
The aesthetic merges **Editorial Modernism** with **Gallery Minimalism**:
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm off-whites, unbleached cotton, and light parchment hues instead of stark cold white.
- **Ink Treatment:** Deep charcoal and onyx text layers that simulate typographic ink pressed into heavy rag paper.
- **Accents:** Earthy terracotta ochre and muted sage olive used strictly as curatorial notation, tags, status indicators, and delicate dividers.
- **Structural Integrity:** Fine 1px architectural hairline borders, generous asymmetrical margins, and rhythmic typographic hierarchies.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from natural pigments, museum gallery walls, and archival print materials. The baseline rests on warm paper textures rather than synthetic digital white, protecting typographic contrast without harshness.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Ochre):** Used for focal anchors, key interactive states, project numbering, and primary callouts.
- **Secondary (`#5A6855` - Muted Sage Olive):** Applied to technical taxonomies, code syntax annotations, and subtle meta tags.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Ochre Gold):** Reserved for achievements, highlights, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#1A1A1A` - Deep Onyx):** Grounding ink tone for headlines, primary prose, and structural dividers.

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Background (`#FAF9F6`):** Primary window canvas mimicking warm museum archival rag.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F4F1EA`):** Subtle layered cards, side drawers, and secondary content planes.
- **Border / Hairline (`#E5E0D5`):** Ultra-subtle, unbleached cotton line work providing structural definition.
- **Muted Ink (`#5C5A55`):** Secondary body copy, timestamps, and architectural captions.

## Typography

Typographic execution centers on the dialectic between classical humanism and Swiss technical calculation.

### Font Roles
- **Playfair Display (Serif):** The editorial voice. Used for major project titles, section overtures, hero announcements, and literary pull quotes. It lends gravitas, artistic maturity, and scholarly presence.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Humanist Geometric Sans):** The narrative voice. Provides warm readability across long-form case studies, project overviews, and personal statements.
- **Inter (Engineered Sans):** The structural voice. Deployed in small-caps or uppercase formats for metadata, code descriptors, indexing figures, system stats, and navigation labels.

### Typographic Rhythm
- Maintain wide, comfortable paragraph leading (`1.65` to `1.85`) to ensure a relaxed reading pace reminiscent of high-end monographs.
- Reserve italic variants of Playfair Display exclusively for project sub-indices, curatorial notes, or translation labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a structured 12-column Swiss grid system, intentionally augmented by asymmetrical white space that mimics gallery curation spaces.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Museum Proportions:** Large margins (`margin-desktop: 5rem`) create an intentional perimeter buffer, isolating the content like a framed canvas.
- **Asymmetric Tension:** Primary content (e.g., project titles and narratives) generally occupies 7 to 8 columns, while technical metadata, git commits, and tech stacks run concurrently along a dedicated 3-to-4 column rail.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max container width 1440px, full dual-rail asymmetric hierarchy.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, consolidated single-column rail with inline metadata chips.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with strictly stacked content cards and sticky pagination headers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic floating elevations and heavy drop shadows, relying instead on planar layering, hairline divisions, and subtle surface tonal shifts.

### Depth Strategy
- **Base Canvas Layer (`#FAF9F6`):** The primary paper substrate upon which all fundamental structural layouts exist.
- **Surface Elevation Layer (`#F4F1EA`):** Cards, project frames, and code inspection blocks sit directly upon the canvas, delineated by a shift in tone rather than elevation blur.
- **Hairline Framing:** Crisp 1px solid borders in unbleached warm linen (`#E5E0D5`) ground cards and separate sections without harsh visual weight.
- **Curatorial Focus Shadow:** When floating elements are unavoidable (e.g., floating image zoom, modal dossiers), use a wide, warm, highly diffused ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(229, 224, 213, 0.6);`

## Shapes

The shape system employs deliberate, understated softening. Corners avoid the cold sharpness of raw industrial interfaces while strictly steering clear of bubbly consumer app silhouettes.

### Corner Archetypes
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, badges, and technical tags. Communicates crisp architectural order.
- **Framed Works & Cards (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to exhibition cards, project viewports, and code preview containers to create a refined matte-board framing effect.
- **Sculptural Details:** Interactive gallery items may feature contrasting corner pairings (e.g., top-left and bottom-right at 12px, opposite corners at 2px) to reference physical architectural models and archival tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Curatorial Action:** Solid Terracotta Ochre (`#C85A32`) background, crisp white text, 4px border radius, uppercase Inter tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`). Hover shifts to a deeper burnt ember (`#B24A24`) with subtle 1px translate up.
- **Secondary / Archival Outline:** Surface `#FAF9F6` background, 1px border in `#1A1A1A`, text in deep onyx. Hover shifts surface to `#F4F1EA`.
- **Text Link:** Deep onyx with a persistent 1px terracotta underline offset by 4px, transitioning to solid terracotta on hover.

### Badges & Taxonomy Chips
- Micro-proportions: 0.6875rem font size, Inter 500, uppercase with wide tracking.
- Background in subtle Sage Olive tint (`rgba(90, 104, 85, 0.08)`), text in `#5A6855`, bordered with 1px hairline (`rgba(90, 104, 85, 0.2)`).

### Exhibition Cards (Projects)
- Constructed with `#F4F1EA` background and 1px `#E5E0D5` outer perimeter.
- Image aspect ratios strictly maintained at classical photographic standards (4:3 or 16:10), bounded by a 12px internal warm canvas matte.
- Card footer splits metadata via an engineered tabular layout: catalog index number (`NO. 01`), tech stack chips, and Playfair Display title.

### Form Inputs
- Minimalist architectural field design: canvas background `#FAF9F6` with an unyielding 1px bottom border in `#1A1A1A` (understated baseline field) or fully enclosed `#F4F1EA` box with `#E5E0D5` perimeter.
- Focused state transitions the border to `#C85A32` with zero box-shadow glow.

### Lists & Timelines
- Clean, ruled-line editorial layout. Items separated by 1px horizontal `#E5E0D5` rules.
- Left column hosts fixed-width monospaced or Inter dates/indices (`2023 — PRES`), right column presents Playfair titles and Plus Jakarta Sans prose.

### Code & Technical Callouts
- Archival code surfaces rendered on light stone paper (`#EFECE4`) rather than harsh dark terminals.
- Syntax highlighting restricted to terracotta, sage olive, and charcoal tones, maintaining the fine-art monograph palette throughout technical artifacts.
